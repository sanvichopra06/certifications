# Virtual Internship – ZIWI Pets, New Zealand (via Talent Solutions) | Education New Zealand Manapou ki te Ao Virtual Internship Program 2026

## Overview

Participated in a 3-week remote, team-based virtual internship addressing a real-world business challenge: assessing ZIWI Pets’ opportunity in India’s premium and ultra-premium pet food market.

## Project Focus

Researched the Indian pet food market and analysed competitor offerings, product positioning, pricing and market trends.

## Areas of Work

- Indian pet food market research
- Competitor analysis
- Product and pricing comparisons
- Regulatory and Import Concerns
- Data analysis and visualisation

## Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Excel

## Key Learning Outcomes

- Gained exposure to industry-oriented market research.
- Developed practical data analysis and business research skills.
- Strengthened professional skills in research, collaboration, communication, problem-solving and presenting evidence-based recommendations.
- Applied analytical thinking to real-world business challenges.
